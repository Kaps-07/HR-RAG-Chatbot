Grievance Redressal Policy

1. Overview
The company is committed to providing a fair, respectful, and inclusive work environment. The grievance redressal policy ensures that all employee complaints and concerns are addressed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees of the company. It covers grievances related to workplace harassment, discrimination, unfair treatment, policy violations, interpersonal conflicts, and any other work-related concerns.

3. Types of Grievances

3.1 Individual Grievances
Grievances raised by an individual employee related to their personal work situation, such as unfair treatment, salary discrepancies, leave disputes, or performance rating concerns.

3.2 Group Grievances
Grievances raised by a group of employees related to a common issue affecting multiple employees, such as changes in working conditions, policy changes, or departmental issues.

3.3 Sexual Harassment Complaints
Complaints related to sexual harassment are handled separately under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy by the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). Refer to the POSH Policy document for details.

4. Grievance Redressal Process

4.1 Step 1 - Informal Resolution
Employees are encouraged to first attempt to resolve the grievance informally by discussing the issue directly with their reporting manager or the concerned party. Most grievances can be resolved at this stage through open communication.

4.2 Step 2 - Formal Grievance Submission
If the grievance is not resolved informally, the employee can raise a formal grievance through the following channels:
- HR Portal: Submit a grievance ticket on the HR portal
- Email: Send a detailed email to hr@company.com
- In Person: Meet with the HR Business Partner for the department

The grievance submission must include the employee's name, department, nature of the grievance, details of the incident, names of persons involved, and any supporting evidence.

4.3 Step 3 - Acknowledgement
HR will acknowledge receipt of the grievance within 2 working days of submission. The employee will be informed of the expected timeline for resolution.

4.4 Step 4 - Investigation
HR will conduct a thorough and impartial investigation of the grievance. The investigation may include interviews with the employee, the concerned party, and any witnesses. All parties involved are expected to cooperate fully with the investigation. The investigation will be completed within 15 working days.

4.5 Step 5 - Resolution
After the investigation, HR will communicate the findings and resolution to the employee within 7 working days of completing the investigation. The resolution may include corrective action, mediation, policy clarification, or other appropriate measures.

4.6 Step 6 - Appeal
If the employee is not satisfied with the resolution, they can appeal to the HR Head within 7 days of receiving the resolution. The HR Head will review the appeal and provide a final decision within 10 working days.

5. Confidentiality
All grievances are treated with strict confidentiality. The identity of the complainant will not be disclosed without their consent. Retaliation against an employee for raising a grievance is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

6. Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
The ICC is constituted as per the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. The ICC handles all complaints related to sexual harassment. The ICC comprises a Presiding Officer, two internal members, and one external member. Complaints to the ICC must be submitted within 3 months of the incident.

7. Timelines Summary
Acknowledgement of grievance: 2 working days
Investigation completion: 15 working days
Resolution communication: 7 working days after investigation
Appeal decision: 10 working days

8. Contact
For grievance-related queries, contact the HR department at hr@company.com or raise a ticket on the HR portal. For sexual harassment complaints, contact the ICC at icc@company.com.
