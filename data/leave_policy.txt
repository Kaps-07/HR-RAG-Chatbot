Leave Policy

1. Overview
The company provides a comprehensive leave policy to ensure employees maintain a healthy work-life balance. All full-time employees are eligible for the following types of leave from the date of joining.

2. Types of Leave

2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave is meant for short-term personal needs such as attending to personal matters, family events, or urgent situations. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and lapses if unused. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leaves can be taken at a time. Casual leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance except in emergencies.

2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave is granted for medical illness or injury. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted to HR within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. Sick leave cannot be encashed.

2.3 Earned Leave (EL)
Employees are entitled to 18 days of earned leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per month. Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 45 days. Earned leave beyond 45 days will be automatically encashed at the basic salary rate. Earned leave must be applied at least 7 days in advance. A minimum of 3 days of earned leave must be taken at a time.

2.4 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children as per the Maternity Benefit Act. For the third child onwards, maternity leave is 12 weeks. Maternity leave can be availed 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery. Refer to the Maternity and Paternity Policy document for full details.

2.5 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave within 30 days of the child's birth. Refer to the Maternity and Paternity Policy document for full details.

2.6 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the HR department.

2.7 Marriage Leave
Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid marriage leave for their own marriage. This leave must be availed within 30 days of the marriage date. Supporting documentation (marriage certificate) must be submitted to HR.

2.8 Compensatory Off (Comp Off)
Employees who work on weekends or public holidays are eligible for compensatory off. Comp off must be availed within 60 days of the working day. Comp off requests must be approved by the reporting manager and submitted through the HR portal.

2.9 Loss of Pay (LOP)
If an employee exhausts all available leave balances, additional leave will be treated as Loss of Pay. LOP will be deducted from the monthly salary at the daily basic salary rate.

3. Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal. Employees must apply for leave at least 3 days in advance for casual leave and 7 days in advance for earned leave. Emergency leave must be communicated to the reporting manager via phone or email immediately, followed by a formal application on the HR portal within 24 hours.

4. Leave Approval
Leave approval is subject to business requirements and manager discretion. The reporting manager must approve or reject leave requests within 2 working days. HR reserves the right to recall an employee from leave in case of business exigency.

5. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year as per the holiday calendar published at the beginning of each year. Employees working on public holidays are eligible for compensatory off or overtime pay as applicable.

6. Leave Encashment
Earned leave beyond the carry-forward limit of 45 days will be encashed at the basic salary rate. Leave encashment is also applicable at the time of resignation or retirement for accumulated earned leave up to the carry-forward limit.

7. Leave Policy for Probationary Employees
Employees on probation are entitled to sick leave only. Casual leave and earned leave will be available after successful completion of the probation period.

8. Contact
For any leave-related queries, contact the HR department at hr@company.com or raise a ticket on the HR portal.
