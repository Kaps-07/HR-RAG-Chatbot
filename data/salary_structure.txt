Salary Structure

1. Overview
The company follows a transparent and competitive salary structure designed to attract and retain top talent. The salary structure is reviewed annually and may be revised based on market benchmarks, company performance, and individual appraisal ratings.

2. Salary Components

2.1 Basic Salary
Basic salary constitutes 40% of the Cost to Company (CTC). It forms the basis for calculating other allowances, provident fund contributions, and gratuity. Basic salary is fully taxable.

2.2 House Rent Allowance (HRA)
HRA constitutes 20% of the CTC. Employees residing in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai) are eligible for HRA exemption up to 50% of basic salary. Employees in non-metro cities are eligible for exemption up to 40% of basic salary. Rent receipts must be submitted to the finance department for HRA exemption claims.

2.3 Special Allowance
Special allowance constitutes 30% of the CTC. It is a flexible component paid monthly and is fully taxable. Special allowance may vary based on the employee's grade and designation.

2.4 Provident Fund (PF)
Provident fund contribution is 12% of the basic salary, contributed equally by the employee and the employer. The employee's contribution is deducted from the monthly salary. The employer's contribution is over and above the CTC. PF is governed by the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

2.5 Gratuity
Gratuity is payable to employees who have completed a minimum of 5 years of continuous service. Gratuity is calculated as (Basic Salary / 26) x 15 x Number of Years of Service. Gratuity is governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

2.6 Performance Bonus
Employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus based on their appraisal rating and company performance. The bonus is typically paid in April after the annual appraisal cycle. Bonus percentages range from 5% to 20% of the annual CTC based on the performance rating.

2.7 Medical Allowance
Employees are entitled to a medical allowance of Rs. 1,250 per month (Rs. 15,000 per year). Medical bills must be submitted to the finance department for reimbursement. Unused medical allowance is paid out as taxable income.

2.8 Travel Allowance (TA)
Employees are entitled to a travel allowance of Rs. 1,600 per month (Rs. 19,200 per year) for commuting to and from the workplace. Travel allowance is exempt from tax up to Rs. 19,200 per year.

3. Salary Disbursement
Salaries are credited to the employee's registered bank account by the 5th of every month. In case the 5th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salaries are credited on the preceding working day. Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 7th of every month.

4. Salary Revision
Salary revisions are carried out annually in April based on the appraisal cycle. The increment percentage is determined by the appraisal rating, market benchmarks, and company performance. Mid-year salary revisions may be considered for exceptional performers or in case of a role change or promotion.

5. Tax Deductions
The company deducts Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) from the monthly salary as per the Income Tax Act. Employees must submit their investment declarations at the beginning of the financial year and actual proofs by January 31st. Failure to submit proofs will result in higher TDS deductions.

6. Salary Advances
Employees may apply for a salary advance of up to one month's net salary in case of a financial emergency. Salary advances are subject to manager and HR approval. The advance will be recovered in equal installments over the next 3 months.

7. Full and Final Settlement
Upon resignation or termination, the full and final settlement will be processed within 45 days of the last working day. The settlement includes pending salary, leave encashment, gratuity (if applicable), and any other dues. Any outstanding loans or advances will be deducted from the final settlement.

8. Contact
For salary-related queries, contact the Finance department at finance@company.com or raise a ticket on the HR portal.
